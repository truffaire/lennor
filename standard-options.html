Option 01

The Ledger

No boxes at all. Four full-width entries on hairline rules, like the index of a specification document. A timber rule sweeps under the row you point at. Calm, confident and it never breaks when the copy lengths differ.

01

Calibrated thickness

Every board is machine-calibrated for uniform thickness across the full sheet — no thick ends, no thin middles.

02

ACC treatment

Boards are dipped in ACC preservative for lifelong resistance against borers, termites and fungal decay.

03

In-house laboratory

Samples from every batch are tested against IS:303 and IS:710 — glue shear, moisture and water-resistance — before dispatch.

04

Pre-cut bulk sizing

For bulk orders we supply pre-cut panels to your dimensions, cutting your wastage and your labour cost on site.
